CASE MANAGEMENT NOTE (SYNTHETIC RECORD)

Discharge planning evaluation:
71-year-old ventilator-dependent man with tracheostomy and PEG after influenza ARDS. Lives with his wife in a single-story home; wife is his healthcare agent. Post-hospital needs: daily ventilator weaning, respiratory therapy around the clock, tube feeding, wound prevention, PT and OT. These needs exceed SNF capability; LTCH level of care is appropriate. Medicare Part A days available.

Met with patient (communicating by writing) and wife. Provided a list of Medicare-participating long-term care hospitals within 60 miles, with Care Compare quality measures for ventilator liberation rates and pressure injury rates for each one, and went over them together. Explained their freedom to choose among the facilities that accept him. Two facilities offered beds; patient and wife chose Pinecrest Specialty Hospital because of its higher ventilator liberation rate.

Discharge Disposition: Long-term acute care hospital (LTCH), Pinecrest Specialty Hospital. Transport by critical care ambulance with respiratory therapist.

Signed by: Odalys Fenwick-Mbeki, RN CM
