Privacy

Draft for the prototype. Have counsel review before any pilot.

  • This prototype accepts only synthetic or de-identified records. Uploads that look like they contain Social Security numbers are refused.
  • Document text, briefs and check results are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes.
  • Every view of a record, upload and check is written to an access log that compliance staff can read.
  • When model review is turned on, record text is sent to Anthropic's API to produce the check and brief. Real patient data would require a Business Associate Agreement first.
  • Records stay within your organization; users in other organizations cannot read them.
