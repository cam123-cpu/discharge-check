Privacy

Draft for the prototype. Have counsel review before any pilot.

  • This prototype accepts only synthetic or de-identified records. Uploads that look like they contain a Social Security number, a date of birth, a medical record number, or a street address are refused. An email or a phone number outside the fictional 555 range is kept only with a warning, and is not sent to the model.
  • Document text, briefs, check results, and the label, unit, attending, disposition, and discharge date are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes.
  • Every view of a record, upload and check is written to an access log that compliance staff can read.
  • Model review is off unless a person asks for it on that check and a key is configured. When it runs, the uploaded text is sent to Anthropic. The model can add a doubt. It cannot mark a pattern miss as shown. Real patient data would require a Business Associate Agreement first.
  • Records stay within your organization; users in other organizations cannot read them.

Terms

Draft for the prototype. Have counsel review before any pilot.

  • You agree not to upload records that identify real patients during the prototype.
